Built for the grooming table

From “can I book?” to “see you in six weeks”

Snoutbook takes care of booking, reminders and the paperwork around every visit, so your day is about the dogs.

Booking page

A booking page that takes bookings while you groom

Share one link. Clients pick a service, choose their pet or add a new one, and take an open time. No more phone tag with wet hands.

  • Prices by size. Set a base price and time for small, medium, large and extra-large dogs.
  • A groomer or “first available”. On Salon, clients choose who grooms their dog.
  • New clients add their own details. Name, phone and email, plus an address for mobile visits.

Reminders & deposits

Reminders and deposits cut no-shows

Every client gets a text and an email 48 hours before the appointment and again 2 hours before. Ask for a deposit when they book online, and a booking means they’re coming.

  • Automatic reminders by text and email. Nothing to send by hand.
  • Deposits through Stripe. Set a fixed amount, like $20, and it comes off the final bill.
  • Rebook reminders six weeks after each visit, or on the schedule you choose.

Pet profiles

Every dog, on file

Breed, weight, coat type, birthday and the notes that matter, with the photos you take on the table. It’s all there before the dog is.

  • Vaccine records for rabies, DHPP and bordetella, with expiry dates and the certificate attached.
  • A red Expired badge on the profile when a record runs out.
  • Temperament notes that travel with the dog from visit to visit.
