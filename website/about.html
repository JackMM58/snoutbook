About Snoutbook

We build the software behind the grooming table

Snoutbook is booking and client software for dog groomers. We started in Portland, Oregon in 2023, and today about 1,400 grooming businesses across the US run on it.

Our story

Snoutbook began with something every groomer already knows: a surprising part of the day goes to the phone. Booking, rebooking, reminding, chasing down vaccine paperwork. Most of it happens with a dog on the table and a dryer running.

Priya Raman and Marcus Bell started Snoutbook in Portland in 2023 to give that time back. We opened a private beta that September with a booking page, a calendar and text reminders, and launched to everyone in January 2024 with the Solo and Salon plans. The Mobile plan followed that April, for groomers who bring the salon to the driveway.

Today about 2,600 groomers use Snoutbook. We’re a remote team of 11 with our home base in Portland, and we still talk with groomers every week about what to build next. You can follow along in our changelog.
