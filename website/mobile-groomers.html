Address, gate & parking notes

Know the driveway before you pull in

Every client has their address and your notes about getting there: which gate, where to park, the latch that sticks. They’re on the appointment every time you visit, for whoever is driving.

  • Client address captured when they book.
  • Gate and parking notes saved on the client, not in your head.
  • Pet profiles and vaccine records, the same as every Snoutbook plan.

Buffer time between stops

Room to pack up and drive

  • Set per van, so each van’s day matches its area.
  • Change it any time as your routes shift with the seasons.
