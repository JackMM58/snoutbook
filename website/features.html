Booking page

Your own booking page, open around the clock

Every business gets a booking link to share anywhere clients find you. Clients pick a service, choose their pet or add a new one, and pick a time that’s actually open. On Salon, they can choose a groomer or take “first available”.

  • Services priced by size. Set a base price and duration for S, M, L and XL dogs, by weight.
  • Add-ons like de-shed, nail grind, teeth brushing and flea bath, added at checkout.
  • New clients sign themselves up with their name, phone and email. On Mobile, they add their address too.
  • Deposits at booking, if you want them. More on that below.

Reminders

Reminders that go out on their own

Snoutbook texts and emails every client before every appointment, so you don’t have to. After the visit, it reminds them when it’s time to book again.

  • 48 hours and 2 hours before, by text and email.
  • A rebook reminder six weeks after each visit. Change the timing to suit your clients.
  • Respectful by default. Clients can reply STOP to opt out of texts.

Deposits

Deposits, taken when they book

Turn on a fixed deposit for online bookings, like $20. Clients pay it with Stripe Checkout as they book, and it comes off the final bill when you check them out.

  • Optional. Turn deposits on or off any time.
  • One fixed amount that you choose.
  • Taken off the final bill automatically at checkout.

Pet profiles & vaccine records

Never get caught by an expired vaccine

Every pet gets a profile with the details you’d otherwise keep in your head: breed, weight, coat type, birthday, temperament notes and the photos you upload. Vaccine records for rabies, DHPP and bordetella sit right there with their expiry dates and a copy of the certificate.

  • Expiry dates on every record, with the certificate file uploaded alongside.
  • A red Expired badge on the pet’s profile once a date has passed.
  • Notes and photos from every visit, so the next groom starts where the last one ended.

Checkout & card readers

Check out at the table

Finish the groom, add any extras, and take payment however the client likes. A deposit paid online is already taken off.

  • Stripe card readers for in-person card payments.
  • Payment links for clients who’d rather pay on their phone.
  • Cash, recorded by hand so your totals stay right.
  • Tips added at checkout.

Card payments are processed by Stripe at 2.9% + 30¢. Snoutbook adds no fee.
