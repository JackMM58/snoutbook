Set up your services

Your services are what clients see and book, so they’re the best place to start.

  1. Open Services and add your first service, for example “Full groom” or “Bath & brush”.
  2. Set a base price and a duration for each size. Sizes go by weight: S, M, L and XL.
  3. Add the rest of your menu. Clients see each service with the price and time for their dog’s size.
For example, a full groom might look like this:
Full groomSMLXL
Base price$60$75$90$110
Duration1.5 hr2 hr2.5 hr3 hr

Add-ons such as de-shed, nail grind, teeth brushing and flea bath are added to the appointment at checkout.

Share your booking link

Every Snoutbook business gets its own booking link.

  1. Open Booking page and copy your link.
  2. Put it wherever clients look for you: your website, your social profiles, your email signature, or a text to your regulars.
  3. Clients pick a service, choose their pet or add a new one, and pick an open time.

New clients give their name, phone and email the first time they book. On the Mobile plan, they also give their address. On Salon, clients can choose a groomer or pick “First available”.

Take deposits

Deposits are optional. When they’re on, clients pay a fixed amount when they book online.

  1. Connect your Stripe account in Settings → Payments.
  2. Turn on deposits and set the amount, for example $20.
  3. That’s it. Clients pay with Stripe Checkout as they book, and the deposit is taken off the final bill at checkout.

Stripe charges 2.9% + 30¢ per card payment, deposits included. Snoutbook adds no fee.

How reminders work

Snoutbook sends reminders for you. There’s nothing to schedule.

  • 48 hours before an appointment: a text and an email.
  • 2 hours before: a text and an email.
  • 6 weeks after a visit: a reminder to rebook. You can change this timing in Settings → Reminders.

Clients who reply STOP to a text won’t get any more texts from your business.

Add vaccine records

Keep each dog’s rabies, DHPP and bordetella records on their pet profile.

  1. Open the pet’s profile and go to Vaccines.
  2. Add each vaccine with its expiry date.
  3. Upload the certificate so it’s on file with the pet.

When an expiry date passes, the pet’s profile shows a red Expired badge, so you know to ask the owner for an updated certificate.

Check your SMS usage

Each plan includes a set number of texts each month: 300 on Solo, 600 on Mobile and 1,000 on Salon.

To see how many texts your business has sent this month, go to Settings → SMS usage. For plan details, see pricing.

Get paid at checkout

When the groom is done, open the appointment and choose Check out.

  • Add any add-ons, then take payment with a Stripe card reader, send a payment link, or record cash by hand.
  • Clients can add a tip.
  • Any deposit they paid online is already taken off the total.

Connect Google Calendar

  1. In Snoutbook, go to Settings → Calendar feed and copy your feed link.
  2. In Google Calendar, add a new calendar from a URL and paste the link.

The feed is one-way. Make changes in Snoutbook, and they show up in Google Calendar.
