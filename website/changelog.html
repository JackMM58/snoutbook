Changelog

What’s new in Snoutbook

New features and improvements to Snoutbook, newest first.

  1. September 2026New

    SMS usage in Settings

    There’s a new page under Settings that shows how many texts your business has sent so far this month.

    Find it at Settings → SMS usage.

  2. July 2026Improved

    A redesigned booking page, and “first available groomer”

    The booking page has a new look, built for clients booking on their phones.

    • Clearer steps: service, pet, then time.
    • Clients can add a new pet without leaving the booking.
    • On Salon, clients can pick “First available” instead of a specific groomer.
  3. April 2026Improved

    New calendar: drag and drop, and week view

    The calendar has been rebuilt from the ground up.

    • Drag an appointment to move it to a new time.
    • Switch between day and week views.
    • Salon calendars keep one column per groomer.
  4. January 2026New

    Add-ons at checkout

    Add extras to an appointment when you check the client out: de-shed, nail grind, teeth brushing, flea bath, and a matting fee.

  5. September 2025New

    Google Calendar feed

    See your Snoutbook appointments in Google Calendar with a one-way iCal feed.

    • Copy your feed link from Settings and add it in Google Calendar.
    • The feed is one-way: changes you make in Snoutbook show up in Google Calendar.
  6. May 2025New

    In-person card payments with Stripe card readers

    Take cards at the table with a Stripe card reader. Payment links and cash are still there for clients who prefer them. As always, Snoutbook adds no fee to Stripe’s.

  7. February 2025New

    Reports

    Salon plans now include Reports.

    • Revenue by day, week and month.
    • Rebook rate.
    • No-show count.
  8. November 2024New

    Rebook reminders

    Six weeks after a visit, Snoutbook now sends the client a reminder to book their next groom. You can change the timing in Settings.

  9. August 2024Improved

    Pet profiles v2: vaccine records and file uploads

    Pet profiles can now hold vaccine records for rabies, DHPP and bordetella.

    • Add an expiry date to each record.
    • Upload the certificate so it’s on file with the pet.
    • Records past their expiry date show a red Expired badge on the pet’s profile.
  10. April 2024Launch

    The Mobile plan

    A new plan for groomers who work from a van, for up to 2 vans.

    • Client address, plus gate and parking notes.
    • Service-area ZIP codes.
    • Buffer minutes between appointments, set for each van.

    Coming soon: route planning. We’re working on putting each day’s stops in order and working out the drive time between them.

  11. March 2024New

    Deposits with Stripe

    Ask for a fixed deposit when clients book online. Clients pay with Stripe Checkout, and the deposit comes off the final bill.

  12. January 2024Launch

    Snoutbook is open to everyone

    After four months of private beta, Snoutbook is available to every groomer, with two plans: Solo for one groomer and Salon for teams. Thank you to every beta groomer who told us what was broken and what was missing.

  13. September 2023Launch

    Private beta

    The first version of Snoutbook goes out to a small group of groomers, with three things: a booking page, a calendar, and SMS reminders.
