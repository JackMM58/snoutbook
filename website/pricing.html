Questions about pricing

Do I need a card to start the free trial?

No. The trial lasts 14 days and you can start it without a card.

How does yearly billing work?

Pay for the year up front and get 2 months free: $290 a year for Solo, $590 for Mobile and $990 for Salon.

Does Snoutbook take a cut of my card payments?

No. Card payments, including deposits, go through Stripe at 2.9% + 30¢ per payment. Snoutbook adds no fee on top.

How do deposits work with the final bill?

When a client pays a deposit at online booking, it’s taken off their final bill at checkout. You choose the amount, for example $20.

How many groomers can use each plan?

Solo is for 1 groomer. Mobile covers up to 2 vans. Salon covers up to 8 groomers, each with their own calendar column.

Can I talk to someone before I choose?

Of course. Get in touch and we’ll help you pick the right plan.
