# Changelog

Customer-facing changes, newest first. Dates are when a release reached every account.

## 2024.03 — 2024-03-12

### Added
- **Deposits**: ask for a fixed deposit when clients book online. It's paid through Stripe Checkout and comes off the final bill.
- **Checkout** with tips. Text the client a payment link, or record cash.
- Connect your own Stripe account. Card payments go straight to you at Stripe's standard rate.

## 1.0 — 2024-01-16

Public launch.

### Added
- **Solo** ($29/mo) and **Salon** ($99/mo) plans, with a 14-day free trial and 2 months free on annual billing.
- Email reminders alongside texts, 48 hours and 2 hours before each appointment.
- Salon: one calendar column per groomer, and staff roles (owner, manager, groomer).
- Texts included each month (Solo 300, Salon 1,000). Extra texts are $0.04 each.

## 0.1 (private beta) — 2023-09-28

### Added
- A booking page for every business.
- Day calendar.
- Text reminders 48 hours and 2 hours before each appointment. Clients can reply STOP to opt out.
