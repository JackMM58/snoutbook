# Changelog

Customer-facing changes, newest first. Dates are when a release reached every account.

## 2026.04 — 2026-04-14

### Added
- New calendar with a **week view**.
- **Drag and drop** to move appointments to another time, or to another groomer's column on Salon.

### Fixed
- Long client names no longer push appointment cards out of their column.

## 2026.01 — 2026-01-13

### Added
- **Add-ons at checkout**: de-shed treatment, nail grind, teeth brushing, flea bath and matting fee. Set your prices in Settings → Services. The matting fee can be set from $15 to $40.

## 2025.09 — 2025-09-09

### Added
- **Calendar feed**: subscribe to your appointments from Google Calendar or any calendar app (read-only iCal). Find your link in Settings → Business.

## 2025.05 — 2025-05-20

### Added
- **In-person card payments** with Stripe card readers. Pick a reader at checkout and the total, tip included, is sent straight to it.

## 2025.02 — 2025-02-18

### Added
- **Reports** on the Salon plan: revenue by day, week or month, rebook rate, and no-show count.

## 2024.11 — 2024-11-12

### Added
- **Rebook reminders**: a text and email 6 weeks after a completed visit with a link back to your booking page. Change the interval or turn them off in Settings → Business.

## 2024.08 — 2024-08-20

### Added
- **Pet profiles v2**: coat type, birthday, and photos you take at the table.
- **Vaccine records** for rabies, DHPP and bordetella with expiry dates and an uploaded certificate. Expired records show a red "Expired" badge on the pet profile.

## 2024.04 — 2024-04-23

### Added
- **Mobile plan** ($59/mo, up to 2 vans, 600 texts a month).
- Service-area ZIP codes. Mobile booking pages ask for the client's address and only accept ZIPs in your list.
- Client address and gate/parking notes, shown on the pet profile and in the calendar feed.
- **Buffer minutes** per van, kept free before and after each appointment for driving and setup.

## 2024.03 — 2024-03-12

### Added
- **Deposits**: ask for a fixed deposit when clients book online. It's paid through Stripe Checkout and comes off the final bill.
- **Checkout** with tips. Text the client a payment link, or record cash.
- Connect your own Stripe account. Card payments go straight to you at Stripe's standard rate.

## 1.0 — 2024-01-16

Public launch.

### Added
- **Solo** ($29/mo) and **Salon** ($99/mo) plans, with a 14-day free trial and 2 months free on annual billing.
- Email reminders alongside texts, 48 hours and 2 hours before each appointment.
- Salon: one calendar column per groomer, and staff roles (owner, manager, groomer).
- Texts included each month (Solo 300, Salon 1,000). Extra texts are $0.04 each.

## 0.1 (private beta) — 2023-09-28

### Added
- A booking page for every business.
- Day calendar.
- Text reminders 48 hours and 2 hours before each appointment. Clients can reply STOP to opt out.
